How to Shoot Clothing Product Photos at Home with a Smartphone

By CropStudio AI Team • Published Aug 2026 • ⏱️ 6 min read

🤖 Key Takeaways (Quick Summary)

  • Lighting: Never use built-in flash. Position flat lays near a north-facing window between 10 AM and 3 PM for soft, diffused light.
  • Camera Settings: Enable 3x3 Grid mode. Use 2x Telephoto lens (or step back) to eliminate lens distortion on garments.
  • Styling: Steam clothes thoroughly—Wrinkles lower perceived value by 40%.
  • Post-Processing: You don't need manual Photoshop editing. Drop smartphone shots into CropStudio AI to convert flat lays into 4K on-model photos instantly.

Step 1: Set Up Natural Window Lighting

You don't need expensive ₹50,000 studio strobe lights to capture high-converting catalog shots. Sunlight is your best free lighting source if positioned correctly.

Place a clean bedsheet or white foam board on the floor 4 to 6 feet away from a window. Crucial Rule: Avoid direct harsh sunlight hit. Soft indirect skylight preserves true fabric colors without blown-out highlights.

Step 2: Smartphone Camera Settings for Apparel

Most smartphone cameras default to wide-angle mode (1x zoom), which bows the edges of shirts and dresses, making them look distorted. Follow these 3 camera tweaks:

  1. Use 2x or 3x Zoom: Step back 2 feet and switch to 2x optical zoom. This flattens the perspective, ensuring shoulders and hem lines look straight.
  2. Turn On Gridlines: Align collar buttons and side seams with vertical grid lines for perfect balance.
  3. Lock Focus & Exposure: Tap and hold on the garment fabric until "AE/AF Lock" appears, then slide exposure slightly down to keep shadows crisp.

Step 3: Garment Prep & Styling

Professional ecommerce studios spend 60% of their time on garment styling. A wrinkled shirt will look unappealing regardless of your camera budget.

  • Hand Steamer: Steam sleeves, collars, and hems right before shooting.
  • Double-sided Tape: Secure loose cuffs or sari pallu edges to the backdrop.
  • Tuck & Pin: Pin excess waist fabric on the back of hangers or flat lays to give garments an athletic, tailored silhouette.

Step 4: AI Post-Processing & Marketplace Compliance

Once you shoot your flat lays or hanger photos, Indian marketplaces like Amazon India, Myntra, and Flipkart require pure white (RGB 255,255,255) backgrounds, strict 3:4 or 1:1 aspect ratios, and minimum 2000px dimensions.
