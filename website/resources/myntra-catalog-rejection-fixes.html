How to Fix Rejected Myntra & Amazon Catalog Product Images

By CropStudio Catalog Team • Published Aug 2026 • ⏱️ 8 min read

🤖 Key Takeaways (QC Diagnosis & Fixes)

  • Rejection Cause 1 (Non-White BG): Off-white backgrounds (e.g., RGB 248,248,248) trigger instant Myntra/Amazon automated QC bots. Fix: Force true RGB 255,255,255 white.
  • Rejection Cause 2 (Margin/Padding Violations): Garments touching image borders or covering >85% of frame get rejected. Maintain 10–15% padding on all sides.
  • Rejection Cause 3 (Wrong Aspect Ratio): Uploading square 1:1 photos on Myntra (requires 3:4 ratio) causes stretching or auto-rejection.
  • Instant Solution: Use CropStudio Marketplace Presets to auto-format aspect ratio, canvas padding, and background RGB in 1 click.

1. Rejection Error: "Background Not Pure White (RGB 255,255,255)"

This is the #1 rejection reason on Amazon India and Myntra. Even if your studio backdrop looks white to the human eye, shadows or camera auto-white-balance often result in light gray (RGB 240,240,240) pixels.

The Fix: Do not attempt manual eraser brush in Photoshop. Use CropStudio's AI Background Remover which automatically cuts the garment edge with sub-pixel precision and replaces the background with pure #FFFFFF hex code.

2. Rejection Error: "Improper Garment Padding / Crop Margin"

Myntra and Flipkart QC algorithms require the garment or model to occupy 80% to 85% of the total frame, leaving an even 10% to 15% breathing margin around top, bottom, and side edges.

If your flat lay touches the edge of the image canvas, the automated scanner marks it as "Cut Off / Poor Frame Framing".

3. Rejection Error: "Low Resolution / Blur (Sub-1000px)"

To enable high-definition image zoom on product pages, Amazon requires main listing images to be at least 1600px to 2000px on the longest side. Myntra prefers 1080x1440px minimum.

If you cropped a smartphone image, the remaining resolution might drop to 800x600px, resulting in a "Resolution Too Low" error.

The Fix: Run your low-res raw photos through CropStudio's built-in AI Upscaler to boost resolution to 4K crispness without pixelation.
