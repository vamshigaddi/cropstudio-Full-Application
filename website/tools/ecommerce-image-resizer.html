Why Marketplace Image Specifications Matter for E-Commerce Sellers

Every major e-commerce platform enforces strict automated Image Quality Checks (QC). If your product photo doesn't match the exact aspect ratio or background luminance, the algorithm deprioritizes your listing or rejects the catalog entirely:

  • Amazon India (1:1 Ratio): Requires a pure solid white background (RGB 255, 255, 255) with the product occupying at least 85% of the frame. Minimum zoom resolution is 1600x1600px (2000x2000px recommended).
  • Myntra (3:4 Vertical): Mandates vertical 3:4 aspect ratio (1080x1440px) with high-fashion models cropped from forehead to lower calf for maximum catalog engagement.
  • Flipkart (1:1 Ratio): Requires high-resolution 1500x1500px square images with clean lighting to enable high-definition hover zoom.
