Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about AI invisible mannequin and neck joint technology.

What is the ghost mannequin effect in e-commerce photography?

Ghost mannequin (also known as invisible mannequin or neck joint effect) makes apparel appear 3D and hollow as if worn by an invisible body. It allows shoppers to see the garment's fit, collar shape, inner lining, and cut without distracting mannequins.

Do I need to shoot a separate photo of the inner back collar?

No! Traditional editing requires two shots (front + turned inside out). CropStudio AI uses generative vision to intelligently reconstruct the inner back collar, brand label depth, and fabric symmetry from a single input photo.

Does the AI add natural shadows or is it a flat cutout?

CropStudio AI renders soft, dimensional studio drop shadows beneath the garment, ensuring your products look grounded, natural, and premium on pure white e-commerce backgrounds.

Which types of garments work best with ghost mannequin?

Ghost mannequin is ideal for formal shirts, t-shirts, blazers, jackets, hoodies, kurtas, and dresses where showing the inner neck curve and garment structure improves buyer trust.
