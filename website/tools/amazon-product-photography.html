Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Amazon product photography with AI.

What is Amazon's main image background color requirement?

Amazon strictly requires the main listing image (hero image) to be on a pure white background with RGB values of exactly 255, 255, 255. CropStudio AI automatically removes distracting shadows and delivers 100% compliant RGB 255,255,255 white backgrounds.

What image resolution and aspect ratio does Amazon recommend?

Amazon recommends a 1:1 square aspect ratio with dimensions of at least 1,600 pixels on the longest side (optimally 2,000 x 2,000 px) to enable their high-converting hover zoom feature. CropStudio AI exports in crystal-clear 2K and 4K resolution.

Can I generate lifestyle and on-model shots for secondary Amazon listing images?

Yes! While Image #1 requires a pure white background, secondary images (Images #2 to #7) convert best with human models wearing your garment in lifestyle settings. CropStudio AI creates realistic Indian and international on-model photos in seconds.

How does CropStudio AI compare to traditional Amazon photography studios in cost?

Traditional e-commerce photography studios charge ₹500 to ₹1,200 per product shoot with 5-7 days turnaround. With CropStudio AI, you can generate 4K marketplace-compliant photos starting under ₹5 per image in 5 seconds.
