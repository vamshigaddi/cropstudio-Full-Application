Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about bulk background removal and e-commerce image isolation.

How many images can I process at once with bulk background removal?

CropStudio AI allows you to drag and drop batches of 50 to 500+ product photos simultaneously. The system processes them in parallel and packages all clean outputs into a single organized ZIP archive.

How does the AI handle transparent fabrics, lace, and hair strands?

Our multi-layer alpha matting neural network detects fine edges with sub-pixel precision, isolating sheer dupattas, lace borders, fringes, and stray hairs without creating harsh or blurry white haloes.

Can I export as transparent PNGs as well as pure white JPGs?

Yes! You can choose lossless transparent PNG (alpha channel) for compositing or pure white RGB 255,255,255 JPG for direct Amazon, Flipkart, and Shopify catalog uploads.

Is there any loss of image quality or resolution during background removal?

No! CropStudio AI preserves 100% of the original photo's pixel resolution and sharpness, with optional 2K and 4K studio upscaling enabled.
