Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about AI ethnic wear and saree photography.

Can CropStudio AI generate saree photos from unstitched fabrics or folded sarees?

Yes! You can upload a folded saree or flat-lay fabric shot on a table. Our AI intelligently projects the pallu, borders, and body pleats onto realistic Indian models with authentic draping physics.

Does the AI support Banarasi, Kanjivaram, Georgette, and Chiffon materials?

Yes! CropStudio's ethnic-wear engine is trained specifically on Indian textiles, preserving metallic gold/silver zari threads, silk luster, transparency of chiffon/organza, and heavy lehenga borders.

Can I choose the model's jewelry, hairstyle, and background?

Yes! You can choose between traditional temple jewelry or modern minimalist styling, varied Indian model facial features, and editorial royal palace or clean studio backgrounds.

Are the exported images ready for Flipkart, Myntra, and Meesho?

Yes! CropStudio allows you to export in 3:4 Flipkart/Myntra portrait ratio or 1:1 square ratio with ultra-sharp 4K resolution that highlights intricate weaving details.
