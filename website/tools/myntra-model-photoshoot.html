Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from fashion brand founders and e-commerce managers.

What aspect ratio does Myntra and AJIO require for catalog listings?

Myntra, AJIO, and Nykaa Fashion require images in a 3:4 portrait aspect ratio (recommended 1080x1440 or 2K/4K ultra HD). CropStudio AI outputs calibrated 3:4 photos with zero cropping distortions.

Can I choose Indian female and male models for ethnic wear?

Yes! CropStudio AI has built-in Indian fashion model presets for women, men, and diverse age groups styled specifically for sarees, kurtis, lehengas, sherwanis, and western wear.

Does the AI preserve original fabric prints and embroidery?

Yes. Our multi-layer neural pipeline isolates and projects your exact garment onto the model while preserving intricate zari embroidery, block prints, textures, and neckline shapes.

Can I process hundreds of fashion SKUs in bulk?

Yes! With our Batch Studio feature, you can upload 100+ raw garment photos, select your desired model and lighting style, and export an entire catalog season in minutes.
