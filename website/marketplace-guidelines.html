Critical Amazon Main Image Rules:

  • The main image MUST show the product on a pure white background (RGB 255,255,255). Off-white or greyish shadows will trigger listing suppression.
  • No text, borders, watermarks, promo badges, or mannequins are permitted in the main hero image.
  • Images must be at least 1,600 px (optimally 2,000 x 2,000 px) to activate the 4x hover zoom feature which increases purchase conversion by up to 35%.

Critical Myntra Fashion Guidelines:

  • Myntra is strictly a 3:4 portrait platform. Square or landscape images will be warped or auto-cropped by Myntra's catalog engine.
  • Apparel listings require on-model photography showing realistic drape, fit, and proportions.
  • Submissions should include at least 4 angles: Front on-model, Back view, Side angle, and a Close-up fabric texture shot.
