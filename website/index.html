Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about CropStudio AI.

Can I use CropStudio photos for Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra listings?

Yes! CropStudio AI outputs images that are 100% compliant with Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, AJIO, and Meesho catalog specifications (pure white backgrounds, 3:4/1:1 ratios, and 4K ultra-high-resolution).

How much money does CropStudio AI save compared to traditional photoshoots?

Traditional fashion photoshoots in India cost ₹500 to ₹1,500 per garment (models, studio rent, photographers, editing). CropStudio AI delivers 4K on-model studio imagery starting under ₹5 per photo.

What type of apparel photos can I upload?

You can upload flat lays on floors/tables, hanger photos, ghost mannequins, or raw smartphone shots of sarees, kurtis, lehengas, shirts, dresses, and t-shirts.

Do I need design or photography skills?

No skills required. CropStudio AI automatically handles background removal, model dressing, realistic fabric folding, studio lighting, and upscaling in 1 click.
